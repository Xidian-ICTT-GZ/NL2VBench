You are given three positive integers: the width of each of two identical closed intervals on the number line, and the starting positions of those two intervals (interpreted as their left endpoints). Compute the minimum non-negative distance that must be applied as a horizontal shift to one interval so that the two intervals touch or overlap. Assume the width and both positions are at least 1.
The result must be zero exactly when the two intervals already touch or overlap, which happens when the absolute difference between the two starting positions is less than or equal to the width. Otherwise, the result equals the absolute difference between the starting positions minus the width. Return the result as a non-negative integer.
fn solve(w: i8, a: i8, b: i8) -> (result: i8)

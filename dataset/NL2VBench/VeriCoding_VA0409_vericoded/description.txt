You are given a line of length n with positions numbered from 1 to n, and two distinct positions a and b on this line. You may perform up to x unit moves, where each move can shift either of the two positions by one step to increase their separation, but positions must remain within the bounds [1, n]. Let the distance between the two positions be the absolute difference of their indices. Compute the maximum possible final distance after at most x moves. Assume 2 <= n <= 100, 0 <= x <= 100, 1 <= a <= n, 1 <= b <= n, and a != b. The returned value must be an integer in the range [0, n - 1]. In other words, the answer is min(|a - b| + x, n - 1).
fn solve_rival_distance(n: i8, x: i8, a: i8, b: i8) -> (result: i8)

There are no additional preconditions on the inputs. The modification must be in place and must not alter the contents of positions other than by replacing occurrences of x with y. Formally, for every index k in the range [0, original_length), if the original element at k equals x, then after the operation the element at k equals y; if the original element at k does not equal x, then after the operation the element at k equals the original element at k.

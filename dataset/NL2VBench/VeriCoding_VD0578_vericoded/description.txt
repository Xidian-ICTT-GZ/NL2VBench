The set of vowels is precisely {'a', 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u'}. Characters not in this set (including uppercase letters and any other symbols) are excluded. The input may be empty. The result, when viewed as a mathematical sequence, must equal the subsequence of the input obtained by filtering out all non-vowel characters according to the given vowel set.
